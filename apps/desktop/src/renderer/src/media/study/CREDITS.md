# Study Room photographs

Rain, Snow, Forest and Night were generated for Kissa with Figma AI.

Fireplace was regenerated on 2026-10-16 with the built-in OpenAI ImageGen tool,
using the previous Figma AI image as the edit target. The 1672 × 941 result was
normalized to 1536 × 864 JPEG (quality 92) for the bundled background.

Prompt: Produce a sharper native horizontal 16:9 photograph. Preserve the quiet
cabin at night, dark stone hearth, small snowy window left, dark furnishings at
the edges, copper-orange firelight and shadowed areas for text. Keep the fire
near the center, natural individual flame tongues, glowing embers, tactile stone
and crisp wood grain. Background photograph only; no UI, text, people or logos.

Animation regions were remeasured in `features/study/scenes/FireplaceScene.ts`.
Sound recording credits are in `sounds/CREDITS.md`.
